Donation V9 – QR-first payment flow

Changes:
- Homepage payment section now opens a dedicated guided donation flow instead of showing the QR immediately.
- Form is a 2-stage flow: donor details -> QR payment + slip upload.
- QR, bank account, PromptPay and note are loaded from donation_settings.
- After returning from a banking app, the page reminds the donor to attach the slip.
- “Transfer completed” sets current date/time and reveals slip upload immediately.
- Donor form draft is preserved in sessionStorage while switching to the banking app.
- No SQL changes required.
